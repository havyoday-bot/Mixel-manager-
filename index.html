What this app does

  • Create Company and Personal projects.
  • Build your own columns and enter data quickly.
  • Search, sort and filter your data, and see totals.
  • Export your data to Excel or CSV files.
  • Take a backup and restore it whenever you need.

Privacy

  • All your data is stored only on this phone.
  • Nothing is sent to any server, and the app's creator never receives your data.
  • Your password is kept only on this phone, in a protected (hashed) form.
  • Files are created only when you tap Export or Backup.
  • Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes your data, so keep a Backup.
